How to Make Sure SpamGuard Emails Show Up in Your Inbox

If you're not seeing emails from SpamGuard — especially if they're landing in your spam folder — here's how to fix it:

✅ For Gmail:
  1. Check your spam folder
  2. If you find a SpamGuard email, click it.
  3. Click the "Not spam" button at the top.
  4. Then:
    • Click the three dots (⋮) on the top right of the message
    • Select "Filter messages like this"
    • In the "From" field, you should see info@spamguardai.com
    • Click "Create filter", then check: "Never send it to Spam"
    • Optionally, apply a label or star
    • Click "Create filter" again to finish.
✅ For Outlook:
  1. Open Outlook
  2. Go to your Junk Email folder and find the SpamGuard email
  3. Right-click it and choose: "Never Block Sender"
  4. Or go to Settings > Junk Email > Safe Senders, and add: info@spamguardai.com

🔒 Bonus Tip: Make sure to add info@spamguardai.com to your contacts to prevent future filtering.
