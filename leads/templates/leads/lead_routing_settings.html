How to Automatically Send SpamGuard Emails to a Specific Folder

Want to keep your inbox clean? Here's how to automatically sort your SpamGuard emails — like spam lead alerts — into a custom folder.

✅ For Gmail Users:
  1. Open Gmail and click the ⚙️ gear icon in the top right, then choose "See all settings".
  2. Go to the "Filters and Blocked Addresses" tab.
  3. Click "Create a new filter".
  4. In the "Subject" field, enter:
    Lead Type: Spam
  5. Click "Create filter".
  6. Check:
    • "Skip the Inbox (Archive it)"
    • "Apply the label" → then create or choose a label like Spam Leads
  7. Click "Create filter" to save.

Now, any email with that subject will go directly into your custom folder.

✅ For Outlook Users:
  1. Open Outlook and click the "Rules" button under the Home tab.
  2. Choose "Manage Rules & Alerts", then click "New Rule".
  3. Select "Apply rule on messages I receive".
  4. Click Next, then check:
    • "With specific words in the subject" → Add: Lead Type: Spam
  5. Click Next, then choose:
    • "Move it to the specified folder" → Choose or create a folder like Spam Leads
  6. Finish the rule to start organizing automatically.

🧠 Tip: We include "Lead Type: Spam" in the subject line of flagged leads so you can sort them easily.

How to Make Sure SpamGuard Emails Show Up in Your Inbox

If you're not seeing emails from SpamGuard — especially if they're landing in your spam folder — here's how to fix it:

✅ For Gmail:
  1. Check your spam folder
  2. If you find a SpamGuard email, click it.
  3. Click the "Not spam" button at the top.
  4. Then:
    • Click the three dots (⋮) on the top right of the message
    • Select "Filter messages like this"
    • In the "From" field, you should see info@spamguardai.com
    • Click "Create filter", then check: "Never send it to Spam"
    • Optionally, apply a label or star
    • Click "Create filter" again to finish.
✅ For Outlook:
  1. Open Outlook
  2. Go to your Junk Email folder and find the SpamGuard email
  3. Right-click it and choose: "Never Block Sender"
  4. Or go to Settings > Junk Email > Safe Senders, and add: info@spamguardai.com

🔒 Bonus Tip: Make sure to add info@spamguardai.com to your contacts to prevent future filtering.
